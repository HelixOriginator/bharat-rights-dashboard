## 🇮🇳 What Is This?

**Bharat Rights Twin** is not a portal. Not a chatbot. Not a report.

It is a **living operating layer** that connects every stage of the citizen-scheme-justice continuum — from constitutional promise to verified delivery — in a single AI-powered interface.

A citizen enters their state, income level, category, and concern. The platform instantly delivers:

- ✅ A personalised **eligibility analysis** across central and state government schemes
- ⚠️ **Exclusion risk detection** — identifying structural barriers before they occur
- ⚖️ **Auto-generated RTI applications** ready to print and submit
- 📋 A **priority action plan** for the next 7 days

No other tool in India chains **policy intelligence → citizen profiling → legal document generation** into one flow.

## 🏛️ Constitutional Foundation

India's Constitution guarantees rights to every citizen. Yet millions of eligible citizens — tenant farmers, MGNREGA workers, students, persons with disability — are structurally excluded from benefits they are legally entitled to, due to:

- Documentation gaps and Aadhaar seeding failures
- Procedural opacity and informational asymmetry
- No structured feedback mechanism when schemes fail
- Legal remedy systems that are inaccessible in practice

**Bharat Rights Twin closes this gap.**

## 📐 Architecture: Three Layers

```
LAYER 01 — SCHEME RIGHTS LAYER
├── Eligibility navigator (plain language)
├── Document checklist with contextual guidance
├── Common exclusion point identification
└── State-specific scheme comparison

LAYER 02 — ACTION LAYER
├── Auto-generated RTI & grievance drafts
├── Legal escalation pathway guidance
├── Multilingual support (planned)
└── Deadline tracking for legal filings

LAYER 03 — GOVERNANCE INTELLIGENCE LAYER (Roadmap)
├── District-level issue heat mapping
├── Anonymised citizen signal aggregation
├── Policy failure pattern detection
└── Civil society & journalist API access
```
